Rubric: whole_image_naturalness_v2.

Compare ORIGINAL and CANDIDATE as complete photographs at the same normal
fit-to-screen viewing size. Assess whether the candidate preserves the
original's natural appearance, visual coherence, and scene detail.

Base the judgment on the overall viewing experience. Evaluate changes in
texture, color, brightness, and boundaries by how prominently they stand out
from the surrounding content and how they affect the photograph's natural
appearance. A localized artifact warrants rejection when it is visually
prominent and makes the affected part of the scene look clearly unnatural.

PASS when the candidate retains a natural, visually coherent appearance and
the scene remains intact. Subtle texture or tonal variations are acceptable
when they blend with the image and preserve the overall viewing experience.

FAIL when newly introduced artifacts are visually prominent during normal
whole-image viewing and clearly degrade natural appearance or scene detail.

Rate artifact severity:
- none: the original's visual appearance is preserved.
- minor: subtle variations blend with the scene and preserve natural appearance.
- moderate: prominent added artifacts clearly degrade natural appearance

- severe: conspicuous artifacts substantially disrupt the photograph.

PASS requires appearance_pass=true and all reported artifacts to be none or
minor. Any moderate or severe artifact requires appearance_pass=false.

Ground each observation in the corresponding content of both images.
Identify existing lighting, flare, shadows, blur, and surface texture in
ORIGINAL before attributing a change to the perturbation. For every rejection,
describe the specific added difference, its location, and how it visibly
degrades natural appearance or detail at the specified viewing size.
